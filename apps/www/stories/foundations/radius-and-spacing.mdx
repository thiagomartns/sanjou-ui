# Radius and spacing

Corners, spacing and shadows are kept few and consistent, so dense screens stay calm.

## Radius

<Canvas of={RadiusAndSpacingStories.Radius} />

All radii derive from one variable, `--radius` (8px), so changing it rescales every corner together. Checkboxes and menu items use `sm`. Buttons, fields, popovers, menus and tooltips use `md`. Cards and dialogs use `lg`, and `xl` is for larger surfaces. `full` is for pills and avatars.

## Spacing

<Canvas of={RadiusAndSpacingStories.Spacing} />

Spacing follows Tailwind's 4px grid: `p-2` is 8px, `gap-4` is 16px. The steps above are the ones exported to Figma and `tokens.json`; stick to them in layouts so screens keep the same rhythm.

## Elevation

<Canvas of={RadiusAndSpacingStories.Elevation} />

Borders separate things; shadows are for what floats. Controls get a hairline `xs` shadow, and only layers above the page (popovers, menus, toasts and dialogs) get `md` or `lg`. In dark mode the shadow color turns black, and borders do most of the work.
